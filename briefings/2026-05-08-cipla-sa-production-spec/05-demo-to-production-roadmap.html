2. SAHPRA SaMD Pathway

ROSE Tier 1 = Class C under MD08-2025/2026 §5.3 (CDSS driving clinical decisions where wrong output could cause significant harm). Tier 4 = Class D (supervising prescribing authority above PCNS independent scope). SAHPRA hasn't commenced product registration under Regulation 1515 / GG 40480, but expects §22C establishment licence + MD08 five-principles compliance today.

2.4 Clinical evaluation requirement

SAHPRA + IMDRF require valid clinical association, analytical validity, clinical performance evidence. The SCGP (~30 consults/week × ~17 wk = ~500-consult cohort, scored against rubric) + benchmark testing process (Goal A/B/C) is the substrate. Subgroup analyses by language (isiZulu, isiXhosa, Afrikaans, English code-switched), region (Gauteng, KZN, WC), condition mix (HTN, T2DM, NIMART, antenatal, paediatric, STI, mental-health screen) required.

2.5 Post-market surveillance

Drift on differential top-3 (weekly, ML Ops); red-flag sensitivity audit (monthly, Clin); override-reason analysis (monthly, Clin+Product); FSCA (as triggered, Reg); PSUR (annual, Reg); complaint log + root-cause (continuous, aggregated quarterly, Reg+QA); cyber vuln scan (quarterly) + pen-test (annual, Security).

2.6 Realistic timeline

12–24 mo §22C-in-hand → full Class C; mid-case 18 mo Tier 1; add 6–12 mo for Tier 4 Class D.

2.7 Interim path

§22C licence (Phase 1) Cipla as licence-holder per Cipla MoU V1.3 Article 10 — required today regardless of registration status. Section 21 unregistered authorisation analogue (Phase 1–2) for programme-level access while Class C TF builds. "Research / SCGP" framing (Phase 1) — structured clinical governance, not general use. TGA Three Criteria precedent: modular IFU argument from tga-cdss-exemption-response-apr2026.md portable to Tier 1; Tier 4 outside.

4.2 Credential gate (per doctor, all hard-gate unless noted)

HPCSA registration current + unblemished (iregister.hpcsa.co.za, monthly recheck) · GP-minimum scope · indemnity ≥ZAR 50M (MPS/PPS/Constantia, annual renewal) · Booklet 10 + 9 + 20 compliance attestations + AI-ethics training · SAPS S91(a) police clearance · right-to-work · 8h ROSE onboarding + competency check · annual HPCSA 30 CEUs · mother-tongue languages (operational, not gating).

4.3 Operational model

Salaried = predictable cost, deep platform knowledge, fungible — anchor pool 4–6 Phase 3. Per-consult contractor = variable, scales with volume, wider pool — surge + Phase 4. Platform-hosted (doctor's own practice + indemnity) = lowest overhead, fragmented governance — Phase 4 SADC. Phase 3 recommendation: hybrid — 4 salaried anchor (Mon–Sat 06:00–22:00 SAST) + 8–12 per-consult contractors. Engineering estimate cost: salary 4 × R900K = R3.6M; contractor R350 × 5,000 = R1.75M; total ~R5.35M/yr (AUD ~$450K) at Phase 3.

4.4 Coverage + escalation

Clinic-hour peaks (Mon–Fri 07:00–17:00) staffed salaried + contractor; after-hours + Sat AM contractor + on-call; nights/Sun on-call only with ER fallback. PCNS → Tier 4: synchronous urgent (chest pain, acute neuro, severe dyspnoea, suicidality, paediatric red flag) doctor within 5 min, system fails over to next-available, then public ER + provincial DoH warm-line at 10 min; asynchronous (Schedule 5 question, ambiguous Dx, follow-up) P95 ≤ 2h SLA; audit-only 10% random sample reviewed in 48h.

4.5 Indemnity + liability

Primary: doctor's MPS/PPS/Constantia ≥ZAR 50M (clinical negligence on Tier 4 advice). Secondary: Botaniqal Pty Ltd PI ≥AUD 20M (engineering estimate; platform error). Tertiary (P3+): reinsurance via Lloyd's or AU PI carrier. Independent: PCNS SANC indemnity (her own practice). Cipla MoU V1.3 + Tshikululu MoU v2.2 leave detailed allocation to Definitive Agreements; engineering target Botaniqal cap ZAR 100M aggregate per-deployment P3+, sub-cap per-incident ZAR 25M.

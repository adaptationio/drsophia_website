v2.0 — 2026-05-08 (afternoon)

Major release driven by multi-agent review of v1.0 plus the AusHealth coverage-gap audit. Adds one new chapter, five new diagrams, two new interactive surfaces, and one new load-bearing finding.

New chapters

  • Chapter 07 — AusHealth Opportunity Stack (~4,800 words). The parallel investor + customer + product track. Covers Stefan Enderling sponsor relationship, Belinda board-recommendation 1 May, 2.5-month DD timeline, indicated Q3 2026 close, the Workplace Health pilot scope (FHIR DiagnosticReport ingestion · D&A test triage · fatigue-risk · RTW sign-off), and the open decisions on AU regulatory pathway (TGA boundary review). Source: wiki/concepts/aushealth-opportunity-stack-may2026.md.

New diagrams (5)

  • D1 · System map (index page) — Cipla / Tshikululu / AusHealth / Botaniqal four-counterparty Mermaid flowchart with colour-coded entity classes (customer / agent / tech / investor).
  • D2 · Apex law tree (Chapter 02) — Constitution + NHA → POPIA / HPA / Nursing Act / Medicines Act → regulators → NDOH frameworks → OHSC enforcement → Sha'p Left clinic. Establishes the cascade for every FR that cites a SA statute.
  • D3 · POPIA §72 cross-border sequence (Chapter 02) — Mermaid sequence diagram showing PHI flow from Patient → Clinic → Botaniqal SA → af-south-1 (Phase 2+) and the de-identified-aggregate path to ap-southeast-2 (Phase 0–1 only). Includes §23 DSAR return path.
  • D9 · 6-phase Gantt (Chapter 05) — May 2026 → 2028+ across all 6 phases plus AusHealth (Phase A) and SAHPRA (parallel) tracks. Anchors Phase Ladder.
  • D10 · 55-risk heatmap (Chapter 05) — Chart.js bubble chart plotting all 55 risks on Likelihood × Impact × inherent-score axes. Top residuals (R-01 / R-02 / R-18 / R-19) labelled. Replaces the wall-of-text register as the primary navigation surface.

New interactive surfaces

  • 8-KPI dashboard (Chapter 04) — SVG gauge cards for differential accuracy / red-flag sensitivity / scope violations / SOAP completeness / discharge note rate / consent capture / breach incidents / nurse adoption. Each tile deep-links to the full KPI definition.
  • SRS live search bar (Chapter 03) — vanilla-JS keyword filter across all 128 FR rows. Types-as-you-go, hides non-matching rows, restores on clear.

New finding

  • Finding 3 — Tshikululu role-conflict. Tshikululu MoU v2.2 simultaneously names Tshikululu as Exclusive Regional Agent (Article 3) and POPIA Sub-operator (Article 18.2). POPIA §20–21 doesn't permit one entity to wear both hats when accepting a DSAR. Implication: pre-12-13 May board, decide between (a) split into two contracting entities (Investments NPC = Agent; Operations Pty Ltd = sub-processor), or (b) restrict Tshikululu's data role to non-PHI metadata only. Newly elevated to P0 in the open-decision register.

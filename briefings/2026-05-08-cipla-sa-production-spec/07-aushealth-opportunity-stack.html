A parallel track to Cipla / Sha'p Left. Three relationship layers under one Adelaide medical-research-charity counterparty (ACNC ABN 85 657 333 858, sole shareholder CALHN). Board recommendation made 1 May 2026; 2.5-month DD open; Q3 2026 close target.

1 · Why AusHealth needs its own chapter

The Cipla / Tshikululu / Sha'p Left stack covered in Chapters 01–06 of this briefing is the South-Africa-side production-spec deliverable. AusHealth is the parallel Australian track — moving on its own timeline, with its own counterparties, its own regulator (TGA), its own customer scope (AusHealth Diagnostics + Acuity Health adjacency), and its own product surface (Rose for Workplace Health).

The Cipla MoU V1.3 capital-stack section names AusHealth in passing. The Tshikululu MoU v2.2 Territory (12 SADC countries, Article 1.7) explicitly excludes Australia. AusHealth is therefore not a sub-tier of the SA stack — it is its own stack. This chapter consolidates it.

6 · Product layer — Rose for Workplace Health

Concept frame: AusHealth Diagnostics extends from D&A testing (current core) into post-D&A-test triage, fatigue-risk assessment, and Return-to-Work (RTW) sign-off — the workflow that sits downstream of D&A collection but is currently under-served because it requires clinical interpretation, not just sample collection.

SaMD classification — TGA implication

This is the single hardest regulatory question in the entire AusHealth opportunity stack, and it is materially different from the Cipla / Sha'p Left Tier 1 classification that landed under the Rule 2.15 CDSS exemption (per concepts/tga-cdss-exemption-response-apr2026.md).

CapabilityLikely TGA classReasoning
FHIR DiagnosticReport ingestion + interpretive triageClass IIa SaMDRule 4.2 (interprets diagnostic info). Outside Rule 2.15 CDSS exemption — output is clinical interpretation of a pathology result, not "general health management information"
Fatigue-risk scoringClass I or IIaRule 4.1 (low-risk, advisory) vs Rule 4.2 (determinative)
RTW sign-offOutside SaMD ladderFinal sign-off is human (AHPRA-registered practitioner countersigning); maps to existing "supportive tool within human-in-the-loop clinical governance framework" disclaimer pattern

Implication: Rose for Workplace Health is more regulatorily-aggressive than either Cipla Tier 1 (CDSS-exempt) or the existing Sophia GP/WL/Alt platform (Class I exempt). Will require a TGA Class IIa SaMD conformity-assessment-body filing (likely 2H 2026 → 1H 2027 timeline; AU$50–150K cert costs; ISO 13485 QMS hardening on top of ISO 27001/27018/27701 we're already trajecting for Tshikululu Article 18.5).

Capital implication: the Layer-3 product cost is therefore not "build it"; it is "TGA-clear it". Wellspring Consortium capital is exactly the right vehicle for this — SA Department of State Development can co-fund SaMD-conformity-assessment-body cost under MedTech / health-tech precincts framing.

7 · The Acuity Health adjacency

This is where the customer (Layer 2) and product (Layer 3) layers concretely converge with a third party.

Acuity Health Australia (a brand of MedHealth Group; Phillip Dilger, pdilger@medhealth.com.au) per Leanne Favero, National Manager CX, 5 May 2026 verbatim:

"Who Acuity Is — we are primarily a medical advisory service, though we don't treat patients, we don't speak to patients and we don't examine patients. We do review existing medical records and provide structured, clinical insight to assist claims managers and their legal panel (defence) with informed, evidence-based decisions. Think of us as 'the medical brain behind an injury claim'."
"Who Acuity's key customers are — Public Liability Insurers, General Insurers, Workers Compensation Insurers, CTP Insurers, Self-Insured Corporations, Legal teams acting on behalf of corporates or insurers."

What we shipped 6 May 2026 demo (live on dev.app.drsophia.link, validated 8/8 Playwright):

  • 5 prepopulated medicolegal containers (Clinical Brief, Diagnosis with [CLAIMED]/[PRE-EXISTING] split, Evidence-by-pillar, Treatment Plan, Medications + Safety Alerts)
  • Live causation Q&A with page-cited responses (real Patient X, redacted, 235 pages)
  • Generate Acuity Report PDF (Iris-template, 17 pages, 286KB)
  • Sophia chat in medicolegal-advisor mode
